ABOUT US

A new chapter starts here.

Starting over can be difficult. No one should have to navigate that journey alone.

The New Leaf Project helps people identify barriers and connect with the people, programs, and resources that can help them build stability, independence, and a brighter future.

We believe that a person's past does not have to determine their future.

HOW WE HELP

Practical support for a fresh start.

We connect people with resources that can help them move forward.

01

Counseling & Support

Providing encouragement, guidance, and support as individuals navigate life's transitions.

02

Documentation Assistance

Helping individuals navigate obtaining driver's licenses, Social Security cards, birth certificates, and other essential identification.

03

Employment & Job Training

Connecting individuals with job-training programs, workforce resources, and employment opportunities.

04

Healthcare Connections

Helping individuals find healthcare resources and connect with appropriate providers.

05

Mental Health Resources

Connecting individuals with organizations and professionals that can provide appropriate mental-health support.

06

Reentry & Community Connections

Helping people reconnect with resources, relationships, and opportunities they need to move forward.

OUR VISION

People are not defined by their past.

We envision a community where people are given meaningful opportunities to create a better future.
